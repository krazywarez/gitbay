#+title: Terms

These are the terms for gitbay.org, kept informal on purpose. Another
instance sets its own.

* What this is

One person runs gitbay.org on one server in Nuremberg, Germany. It is
free, comes with no warranty, and makes no uptime promise. Repositories
are backed up nightly off the host, but keep your own clone of anything
that matters.

* What is not allowed

- malware, phishing kits, or anything built to attack other systems
  (security research and proof-of-concept code with a clear purpose are
  fine);
- spam, including accounts or repositories that exist to carry links;
- content that is illegal in Germany, or that infringes someone's
  copyright;
- harassment of other users;
- using CI, storage or bandwidth as general-purpose hosting: crypto
  mining, file dumps, or mirrors of large binaries unrelated to a
  project.

* Limits

Each account may own 250 repositories and 5 GB. An organization has
the same caps, and an account may create 5 organizations. Ask if you
need more.

* What the operator may do

Remove content, archive a repository or make it private, or disable an
account, when it breaks the rules above or puts the instance at risk.
Reports go to the address on the [[Abuse][abuse page]].

* Leaving

=account export= writes your profile, repositories, issues and merge
requests as a bundle another gitbay instance can replay
(=gitbay migrate=), and every repository can be cloned. To have
the account deleted, mail the abuse address from the account's primary
email.

If gitbay.org ever shuts down, every account with a verified address
gets notice and time to export first.

* Privacy

See https://gitbay.org/privacy.
