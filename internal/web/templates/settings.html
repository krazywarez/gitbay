Checked before a merge, in this order: checks, approvals, resolved threads, signatures.

Requires CI to succeed.{{with .Repo.Settings.RequiredContexts}} Also waits for {{range $i, $c := .}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$c}}{{end}} until they report{{if not $.Repo.Settings.RequireChecks}}, once this is on{{end}}.{{end}}
